Who We Are

MDtech-Solutions was founded on a simple idea: homes and businesses deserve reliable, affordable tech solutions delivered with professionalism.

From CCTV surveillance and computer systems to solar power, UPS backups, and solar streetlights — we've grown into a full-service technology partner.

Our team of certified technicians has delivered over 500 projects across residential, commercial, and institutional clients. Every project is handled with the same care, precision, and after-sales support we'd want for our own families.
